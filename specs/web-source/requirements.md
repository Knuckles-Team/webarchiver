| `CONN-WEBARCHIVER-WEB-SOURCE-R001` | **Pages answer through the web-source contract (rollup).** The webarchiver connector must implement the web-source contract of `SCION-SOURCE-INGEST-R005.40`. It serves the per-operation tools `web_fetch`, `web_extract`, `web_render` and `web_screenshot`. It is the primary backend of `research.web` (`ARBORA-OPS-R067.3`). Children `.1` to `.8` and `.10` deliver it. | See each child. |

They take a `WebFetchRequest` and return a `WebPageResult` or a `WebFetchRefusal`, with `backend=webarchiver`. `web_render`, `web_screenshot`, `web_links` and `web_crawl` are the connector's own per-operation tools. | `CONN-WEBARCHIVER-WEB-SOURCE-R001.2` | **Behavior.** The connector must obey `robots.txt` for its user agent and cache each file for 3600 s. It must keep a per-domain rate limit of 1 request per second by default. It must stop reading at `max_bytes` and refuse an unlisted content type. `web_extract` must reduce the page to its main content as markdown. It must return the title, author, published date, canonical URL and outbound links. `content_digest` is the SHA-256 of the extracted text. Caller chain: the `webarchiver-mcp` console script runs `webarchiver.mcp_server:mcp_server`, which calls `register_web_source_tools` (webarchiver/mcp_server.py:46) over `WebarchiverSource` (webarchiver/api/web_source.py:31); the fetcher in `webarchiver/api/fetcher.py` applies robots, rate and size policy of `webarchiver/api/policy.py`. Shape: `webarchiver/api/fetcher.py`, `webarchiver/api/policy.py` and `webarchiver/api/extraction.py`. Proving test: `tests/test_extract.py::test_fetch_follows_redirect_and_returns_html`. | Fixture pages yield the expected markdown and metadata, and a disallowed path is refused. |

| `CONN-WEBARCHIVER-WEB-SOURCE-R001.4` | **Refusal.** Each refusal must use a closed reason of the contract: `robots_disallowed`, `rate_limited`, `size_exceeded`, `unsupported_type`, `egress_denied`, `policy_refused`, `extract_empty` or `upstream_failed`. | `CONN-WEBARCHIVER-WEB-SOURCE-R001.7` | **Bounded site crawl.** `web_crawl(seed_url, same_site, max_pages, max_depth, include, exclude)` in `webarchiver/api/crawl.py` must walk links breadth-first from one seed and return the page URLs it reached, in discovery order. It must fetch every page through the same policy as `web_fetch`: robots.txt, the per-domain rate limit, size and content-type caps and egress pinning. `same_site` defaults to true. `max_pages` ranges from 1 to 1000 and `max_depth` from 0 to 10. `include` and `exclude` are regular expressions. A robots-disallowed or failed page is reported as skipped with its closed reason. It returns URLs only; fetching content is the caller's next step. Proving test: `tests/test_crawl.py::test_bounded_crawl`. | A fixture site yields the expected URLs in order, the disallowed path is reported and never fetched, and out-of-range bounds refuse with `policy_refused`. |
